Alstack product / EMI Tracker

Responsive web application

Manage every loan and EMI in one place.

EMI Tracker consolidates loan obligations, contractual repayment schedules, payment status and portfolio progress so users can see exactly what is due, what is paid and what remains.

Live demoRequest product walkthrough

What it solves

A single view across fragmented loan obligations

Instead of checking multiple lender statements and manually tracking dates, users can maintain one portfolio with consistent repayment visibility.

1

Contractual repayment schedules

The First EMI Due Date comes from the lender agreement or statement. EMI Tracker does not guess contractual dates for consumer-entered loans.

2

Clear remaining obligations

Track outstanding principal, remaining interest and Remaining EMI Obligation across the repayment lifecycle.

3

Payments and loan lifecycle

Record installment payments, follow repayment progress, surface overdue items and transition fully paid loans to Completed automatically.

4

Portfolio analytics and reports

Dashboard metrics, loan-level reporting, portfolio completion and PDF / Excel / CSV exports support day-to-day tracking and review.

Core capabilities available today

Ready for a client demonstration

Loan portfolio

Multiple loans, lender details, tenure, interest rate, contractual First EMI Date and repayment source.

Repayment schedules

Principal and interest split, opening balance, monthly installments, month-end handling and zero-interest schedules.

Payments

Pending, paid and overdue installments with payment history and automatic loan completion.

Notifications

Activity feed, reminders and payment updates with read/unread state management.

Reports

Portfolio and loan analytics with export to PDF, Excel and CSV.

Account security

Authentication, profile controls, session revocation and secure single-use password recovery.
