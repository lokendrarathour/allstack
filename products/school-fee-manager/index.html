What it solves

Replace fragmented fee records with one controlled operating system

School Fee Manager is designed to connect academic structure, student fee obligations and day-to-day collections without losing the financial history needed for reconciliation and audit.

1

Academic foundation

Create the academic structure used by fee operations, including academic sessions, classes, sections and student activation.

2

Fee configuration

Define fee heads and fee structures, then connect the appropriate charges to the students and academic context they belong to.

3

Collections and allocation

Record payments through controlled collection workflows, allocate money against the correct obligations and retain an operational payment trail.

4

Receipts, reconciliation and reporting

Generate receipt records, manage cashier sessions and use reports to review collection activity and outstanding school fee positions.

Financial control

Designed for traceability, not spreadsheet-style rewriting

School finance data needs clear ownership and durable history. The product keeps school data tenant-scoped and maintains one authoritative financial record so collections, receipts and historical activity remain traceable and consistent.

Tenant-scoped data

School operations are scoped to the authenticated school context so one tenant's data is not treated as another tenant's workspace.

Financial history preserved

Financial history is designed around recorded events, with reversals and refunds treated explicitly instead of casually editing or deleting past transactions.

Receipt integrity

Receipts are treated as durable financial snapshots so the record presented to the school can remain traceable to the underlying collection event.
